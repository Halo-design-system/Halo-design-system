# Words on screen

Halo screens use plain, short, specific words. Write the way a helpful person would say it.

## Everywhere

- Sentence case: "Create project", not "Create Project".
- Short, everyday words. "Use", not "utilise"; "start", not "initiate".
- No em dashes. Use a full stop, a colon or a comma.
- Don't name the product in example text unless the user asked for it.

## Buttons and links

- Start with a verb and say what happens: "Save changes", "Delete project", "Send invite".
- Never "OK", "Submit", "Click here" or "Yes".
- Match the button to the question: a Modal asking "Delete this project?" has "Delete" and "Cancel".
- Links say where they go: "View all invoices", not "More".

## Titles and descriptions

- A page title names the thing: "Billing", "Team members".
- A description says what people can do here, in one line.
- A Modal title is a question when there's a decision: "Delete project?".

## Errors

- Say what happened and how to fix it: "Use at least 8 characters." "Your card was declined. Try another card."
- Put the error next to the field it's about. Don't blame people.

## Empty states

- Say what's missing and what to do: "No projects yet" and "Create a project to start organising your work."
- Add a button only when there's a clear next step.

## Confirmations

- Toasts say what happened, in the past tense: "Changes saved", "Invite sent". Add Undo when it can be undone.
